The Nigeria DHS microdata are not distributed with this repository.

Authorised users wishing to reproduce the analysis should obtain access
to the 2024 Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey through The DHS Program,
download the Pregnancy and Postnatal Care Recode in Stata format,
extract the authorised .DTA file, and place it in this directory.

The original respondent-level data remain subject to The DHS Program's
terms of use.
